For Development Companies

Business development for embedded and software companies

You build the boards, the BSPs and the software. We bring the customers. Synergy-ED works as a business development team for development companies in the Israeli embedded sector, from startups to large enterprises.

The Problem

Good engineering should not depend on the founder's sales calls

Most development houses win work through reputation and word of mouth. That works until it stops working: the network that fed the company at five people does not feed it at twenty, and the founders end up splitting their week between delivering projects and chasing the next one.

Cold outreach rarely fixes it. Technical companies sell to technical buyers, and a generic sales email from an unknown address goes straight to the trash. What works is someone who already knows the sector, knows who is looking for a team like yours, and opens the door with a warm introduction.

What we take on as your business development team

  • Mapping the Israeli embedded market for your specific offering: development services, embedded software, BSP and firmware, or full turnkey.
  • Warm introductions to companies that need what you build, from startups to large enterprises.
  • First calls and meetings arranged and followed up, together with you or on your behalf.
  • Positioning and messaging aimed at technical buyers, in English and in Hebrew.
  • Ongoing consulting on proposals, pricing and partnership structures.
How We Work

One partner that speaks engineering and business

We come from the embedded world ourselves, so we can present your work to a CTO and to a CEO in the same week. Tell us what you build and which customers you want to reach. We map the relevant companies, make the introductions, and stay involved through the first projects.
